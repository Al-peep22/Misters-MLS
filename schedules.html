Export JSON backup

Download one JSON backup containing employees, availability, time off, and shifts for the currently selected week. Import merges employees by ID and replaces the currently selected week's schedule; other weeks and local-only preferences are left unchanged. Unlock the selected week before importing.

The backup includes employee details, availability, time off, and schedule data. To remove an employee or clear information, make that change in the app before exporting. Preferences remain saved locally and are not included in the backup.
